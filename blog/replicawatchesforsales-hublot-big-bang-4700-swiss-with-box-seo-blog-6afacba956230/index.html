Hublot Big Bang 4700 Swiss With Box: A Bold Skeleton Chronograph Look

Editorial Team
View profile

Introduction: A Bold Skeleton Chronograph Aesthetic

The Hublot Big Bang family is globally associated with an audacious and confident sports-watch design language: a substantial multi-piece case, visible bezel screws, modular construction, and a sharp industrial contrast between metal, technical dark surfaces, and vulcanized rubber. The Hublot Big Bang 4700 Swiss With Box product page presents a timepiece inspired by this assertive visual style, combining high-octane chronograph styling with a dedicated presentation box.

Product Transparency Note: This item is offered within an alternative luxury/replica catalogue. References to “Swiss”, “ETA”, or “4700” in the product narrative describe design inspiration and mechanical architecture rather than independent manufacture by Hublot.

Case Architecture & Materials

The listing’s technical specification panel states a durable 904L stainless-steel case measuring a versatile 41 mm in diameter. 904L grade stainless steel provides exceptional structural rigidity, corrosion resistance, and a refined brushed-and-polished contrast that holds up well under daily wear.

The case is paired with an industrial black fixed bezel accented by signature structural screws, a scratch-resistant front sapphire crystal with anti-reflective treatment, and dual chronograph pushers flanking the knurled crown. The seller states water resistance of up to 50 metres (5 ATM); as with any sports timepiece, verifying gasket integrity and avoiding water exposure prior to pressure testing is recommended.

Dial & Visual Detailing

The dial serves as the visual highlight of the Big Bang 4700 design. It features a textured, multi-layer semi-skeletonized layout with recessed chronograph subdials, creating an instrument-inspired aesthetic reminiscent of motorsport telemetry. Applied luminous hour markers and skeletonized hands provide sharp legibility against the complex technical background, complemented by Super-LumiNova luminescence for reliable low-light visibility.

Movement Mechanics & Chronograph Functions

Inside the timepiece beats an automatic self-winding chronograph movement engineered upon proven mechanical architecture. The movement drives running hours, minutes, and subsidiary seconds alongside an active chronograph function operated via the pushers at 2 and 4 o'clock for start, stop, and reset operations.

Strap, Clasp & Wearing Style

Fitted to the case is an ergonomic black textured vulcanized rubber strap, designed to seamlessly contour to the wrist while providing heat and sweat resistance. The strap is secured with a reinforced deployant folding safety clasp.

The bold sports-watch styling pairs effortlessly with casual and smart-casual attire: charcoal, black, and navy casual tailoring, leather jackets, or a simple dark tee and raw denim, allowing the assertive 41 mm case architecture to command attention.

The Presentation Box & Collector Considerations

A defining inclusion highlighted in this specific listing is the presentation box set. The watch comes accompanied by a branded display box, providing an elegant storage solution and an elevated unboxing experience suitable for gifts or personal collection displays.

Buyer Considerations & Summary

When evaluating this timepiece, key specifications to note include:

  • Case Build: 41 mm 904L stainless steel with black bezel accents.
  • Dial Layout: Multi-layer skeleton chronograph with high-contrast registers.
  • Inclusions: Complete with presentation box set.
  • Support: Backed by the seller's standard one-year warranty and tracking support.

Conclusion

The Hublot Big Bang 4700 Swiss With Box delivers an assertive sports-luxury aesthetic: an unmistakable bezel silhouette, complex multi-layer dial, reliable automatic chronograph mechanics, and complete presentation packaging. For style enthusiasts who appreciate the cutting-edge aesthetic of modern fusion horology, it offers a standout presence on the wrist.
